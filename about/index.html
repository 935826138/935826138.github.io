About TraderPilot

We're a team of real traders who got tired of fake broker reviews. Every broker on this site has been tested with real money. We show you the good, the bad, and the ugly.

Why We Started

Most forex review sites are affiliate farms — copy-paste content written by people who've never placed a trade. We built TraderPilot to publish honest, hands-on broker assessments from traders who actually use these platforms daily.

How We Review

  • Real money accounts — no demo reviews
  • Live spread tracking — during London and NY sessions
  • Withdrawal testing — we test how fast money comes back
  • Regular updates — brokers change, so do our reviews

How We Get Paid

We earn affiliate commissions from some brokers when you open an account through our links. This does not affect our ratings. We have turned down brokers who offered high commissions but had poor execution or shady practices.
